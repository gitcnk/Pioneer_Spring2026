### Final Paper Guidelines:


- Do not worry too much about the page count or word count.  Make sure your analysis is solid and you interpret your findings.


- This folder also contains a paper written by two of my students few years ago.  You can use it as a guide to structure your final paper.


- If you'd like to use LaTex (highly recommend) you can use the following journal article template: https://www.overleaf.com/project/69de8352fea55d39f92b9bf9

**Note: You'll need an account from Overleaf (https://www.overleaf.com/) to open this.  Make sure to register for a free account beofore you open this**
